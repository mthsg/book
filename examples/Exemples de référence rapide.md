## PART 1: UNDERSTAND YOUR ECOSYSTEM

### Ch. 1: Mapping the Terrain
**Qonto** → How to size TAM precisely (€52B total SME finance market; 21M SMEs; Qonto = 3% penetration)
**Back Market** → How to discover profit pools in "waste" categories (53.6M tons e-waste; only 17% recycled)

### Ch. 2: Enemy is Inaction
**Qonto** → Real competitor is Excel + accountant (5-8 hours/month friction), not Revolut
**Personio** → 70% of European SMEs still use spreadsheets for HR; that's your market entry point

### Ch. 3: Who Signs the Check?
**Alan** → Buying committee has 4 personas (HR, Finance, CEO, Employee) with different emotional drivers
**Swile** → The buyer (Finance, cost-cutting) is different from the champion (HR, culture-building)

### Ch. 4: Economic Model
**Qonto** → Unit economics: €15-25 ARPU, <2% churn, 110%+ NRR, 3-month CAC payback = Magic Ratio
**Back Market** → B2C margin (15-20%) vs. B2B margin (25-35%); channel mix impacts overall profitability

### Ch. 5: Pricing as Product
**Alan** → Good-Better-Best: Core (€1.50) → Better (€2.20) → Best (€3.00+) per employee/month
**Qonto** → Start low (€9 solo) then expand pricing: €9 → €29 → €29+€5 (invoicing) → €29+€15 (spend mgmt)

### Ch. 6: Distribution
**Miro** → PLG playbook: Freemium + unlimited users + 1K templates + viral invites = 500% growth (5M→30M users)
**Qonto** → Hybrid: Foot-in-door (€9 solo) + direct (SME sales) + partners (accountant commission)

### Ch. 7: Revenue Roadmap
**Qonto** → Explicit anti-scope: NOT building lending, insurance, or accounting software. Just financing partnerships.
**Back Market** → Core marketplace → B2B added → Retail stores (phased expansion, not all at once)

### Ch. 8: Narrative
**Alan** → Repositioned from "health insurance" → "healthcare operating system" (emotional + categorical)
**Back Market** → Repositioned from "second-hand electronics" → "sustainable refurbished tech" (values-driven)

## PART 2: LINK YOUR PRODUCT AND YOUR BUSINESS

### Ch. 9: GTM Readiness
**Qonto** → Sales enablement: Different narratives for SME self-serve vs. accountant partner channel
**Alan** → Objection handling scripted: "Healthcare is complex" → "We make it simple + personalized"

### Ch. 10: Orchestrating Launch
**Back Market** → Retail store launch required work-back timeline: 12-month arc from concept → store opening
**Key checklist**: Strategy ✓ Product Readiness ✓ Billing ✓ Content ✓

### Ch. 11: Demand Engine
**Miro** → Organic growth via 1,000+ templates + SEO content + webinars (no big ad spend; 500% growth via word-of-mouth)
**Loop 1**: Share board → friend sees value → signs up → invites 5 more (viral)
**Loop 2**: Template signup → quick win → word-of-mouth

### Ch. 12: Activation & Expansion
**Alan** → Onboarding: Admin sets up in 10 min → Employee activates in 5 min → Habit forms in 2-3 weeks
**Expansion triggers**: Mental health adoption <20% → Upsell premium; Telemedicine >10/month → Offer specialist care

### Ch. 13: Feedback Loop
**Qonto** → Prioritization: Support tickets (high signal) + Churn surveys (high) + Feature requests (medium) + Sales objections (low)
**Example**: "Invoicing requests 200x/month" → High signal → Rank #1
**Example**: "Mobile app requests 50x/month" → Low signal → Deprioritize

### Ch. 14: Operating Rhythm
**European SaaS Composite** → Monthly dashboard: MRR + churn + NRR + Rule of 40 (Growth + FCF ≥ 40%)
**Quarterly**: Expansion review by cohort; Churn postmortem by reason; Financial forecast (fundraising?)
